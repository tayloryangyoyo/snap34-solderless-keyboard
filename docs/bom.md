# Bill of Materials

Quantities are for one complete split keyboard.

| Item | Qty | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Custom PCB, left half | 1 | Order with assembly service for a solderless end-user build. |
| Custom PCB, right half | 1 | Mirror of left half. |
| MX hot-swap sockets | 34 | Kailh-style sockets, assembled by PCB vendor. |
| 1N4148W SMD diodes | 34 | One per switch, assembled by PCB vendor. |

| TRRS jack or USB-C interconnect | 2 | Pick one interconnect strategy and keep both halves consistent. |
| MX switches | 34 | Any plate-mount or PCB-mount MX-compatible switch. |
| 1u keycaps | 34 | Thumb keys can use 1u or 1.25u if the plate is revised. |
| Top plate | 2 | 1.5 mm FR4, 1.5 mm aluminum, or 3 mm acrylic. |
| Bottom plate | 2 | FR4, acrylic, or 3D printed shell. |
| M2 standoffs | 12 | 6 per side, 6 mm to 8 mm typical. |
| M2 screws | 24 | Match standoff length and plate thickness. |
| Rubber feet | 8 | Optional but recommended. |

## Sourcing Notes

- For true no-solder assembly, choose a PCB vendor that can populate the hot-swap sockets and controller sockets.
- If the vendor cannot assemble the controller sockets, use a controller carrier board that ships pre-soldered.
- Ask the PCB vendor to confirm hot-swap socket orientation before production.
- Test-fit one switch in the plate before committing to a full production run.
